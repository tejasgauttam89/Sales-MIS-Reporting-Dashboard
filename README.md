# 📊 Sales MIS Reporting Dashboard

### Business Sales Performance Analysis & Reporting

A Sales MIS Reporting Dashboard designed to analyze business sales performance, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), track revenue trends, and generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data and presenting business performance through structured reports and visual dashboards.

The dashboard helps users understand sales trends, evaluate performance, and make data-driven business decisions.
